RSS podcast feeds generated from the IIMS podcast archive.

How to subscribe

  1. Copy the feed URL you want (tap the Copy button next to it below, or long-press the feed link and choose “Copy link address”).
  2. In your podcast app, use “Add podcast” → “Add by RSS URL” (AntennaPod, Pocket Casts, Podverse, ...) or “Add a Show by URL” (Apple Podcasts) and paste it.

If tapping a feed link downloads a file instead of opening your app, that is your browser being cautious with XML files — copying the link and pasting it into the app always works. On apps that support the podcast: link scheme, the Subscribe links below open the subscription directly.
